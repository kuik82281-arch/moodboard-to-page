# Style brief: French romantic (法式浪漫 · 慵懒与优雅)

References (typical): a Parisian café, toile de Jouy, pastry boxes, handwritten letters in ink, pressed flowers, Rohmer films, linen and lace.

| Dimension | What I see | For the page |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Linen, cotton paper, lace, porcelain, gilded frames, silk ribbon | Soft paper cards, lace edges, a gold hairline frame |
| Edges | Scalloped, deckled, oval frames, ribbon ends | Scalloped / oval frames for photos; deckled letter paper |
| Layering | Gentle: a letter on a tablecloth, a flower laid across, a ribbon tied | Few layers; one ribbon or sprig crossing a card |
| Density | Airy, unhurried | Lots of space; centred compositions allowed |
| Text as texture | Handwritten French in ink, toile patterns, menu du jour | Faint script in the ground; toile on one surface only |
| Typography | Script, Didone, elegant italic | Display: Savoye LET / Snell Roundhand; serif: Didot / Baskerville; body: Songti SC light |
| Colour | Powder pink, cream, sage, French blue, gold; or toile blue on cream | Ground #f7f0e8; ink #3b3433; accents #e7b7b2, #7c93b8, gold #b8955a |
| Motifs | Bows, roses, café chairs, striped awning, wax seal, macarons | Ribbon bows, wax seals, striped awning header, pressed flowers |
| Light & depth | Soft daylight, film softness | Soft long shadows; a little warm glow |
| Motion | Lazy: a petal drifting, a ribbon swaying | Slow sway, long easing |
| Breakage | Almost none; lived-in softness (a wine ring, a creased letter) | Local softness, never torn |
| Integration | Letters, menus and cards laid on linen | Content written on letter paper and menu cards; ornaments laid on top |

**Signature** - script titles with a ribbon or wax seal; powder pink, French blue and gold hairlines; toile or awning stripes.
**Never** - hard black, neon, grunge, crowded layouts.

## Kinds by function
chat → letters sealed with wax · calendar → café menu du jour · gallery → gilded salon frames · settings → a dressing-table
card · music → a gramophone on a balcony.

## Hero and lines
Hero: the Eiffel Tower over the Seine at dusk as a copperplate etching tinted in watercolour (Haussmann roofs, plane
trees, Pont Alexandre III with candelabra) - see `scripts/examples/paris_etching.py`. Other heroes: Montmartre steps,
a café terrace, the Louvre at night. Lines: musical staves (a chanson), guinguette string lights, balcony ironwork,
bridge balustrades, postmark waves. Avoid the cheap kit: lone bows, balloons, plain gilt boxes.
