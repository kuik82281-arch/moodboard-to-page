# Style brief: Korean soft (韩系 · 柔软)

References (typical): cream and milk-tea palettes, small bears and ribbons, sticker sheets, frosted acrylic, lots of air.

| Dimension | What I see | For the page |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Matte paper, frosted acrylic, plush, ceramic | Cards as soft matte tiles or frosted acrylic, never textured grunge |
| Edges | Large round corners, die-cut sticker outlines with a white border | Radii 20-28px; stickers with 3-4px white outline |
| Layering | Few layers, floating gently, generous gaps | Flat layout with occasional floating stickers |
| Density | Airy, calm, much empty space | Wide margins, one idea per block |
| Text as texture | Almost none; tiny English labels at most | No filler text |
| Typography | Round sans, thin weights, small caps labels, cute handwriting accents | Display: rounded sans; body: PingFang / SF Rounded light |
| Colour | Cream, milk tea, butter, baby blue, dusty pink; low saturation | Ground #fbf6ef, ink #5b4a42, accents pastel |
| Motifs | Bears, ribbons, hearts, clouds, checks, stars | Small stickers, used sparingly |
| Light & depth | Soft long shadows, slight glow | Very soft shadows (0 8px 24px alpha .06) |
| Motion | Bouncy, gentle | Spring scale on tap, slow float |
| Breakage | None: everything whole and neat | Never tear, never crumple |
| Integration | Clean objects floating on an empty ground; stickers stuck on with a white rim | Soft cards may float (that is the style); stickers sit on them |

**Signature** - airy cream ground; round soft cards; tiny cute stickers with white outlines.
**Never** - grunge, tears, dark heavy ink, crowded layouts, sharp corners.

## Hero and lines
Hero: one big soft scene - a cloud-filled window, a cafe counter with cakes, a bedroom shelf of plush toys. Lines: knitted yarn, ribbon with a bow end, dotted stitch lines, a strand of pearls, washi tape.
