# Style brief: Hong Kong (港风 · 霓虹与旧港片)

References (typical): Wong Kar-wai film stills, neon signs over Mong Kok, cha chaan teng menus, minibus signs, 90s film posters, the tram.

| Dimension | What I see | For the page |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Wet night street, neon glass tubes, film grain, Formica tables, enamel signs | Dark ground with grain; signs as glowing or enamel panels |
| Edges | Sign boxes, rounded neon tubes, menu frames with double rules | Hard rectangles, neon outlines (stroke plus glow) |
| Layering | Signs stacked over each other, vertical boards crossing the street | Vertical labels, overlapping sign panels at different depths |
| Density | Crowded signage up top, calm faces in the middle | Busy edges, calm centre for the content |
| Text as texture | Traditional Chinese everywhere, vertical, bilingual | Vertical 繁體 labels, small English beneath, menu prices |
| Typography | Bold Ming / Hei on signs, brush on menus, Song on film posters | Display: Songti TC bold / Heiti TC bold; accent: Kaiti; English: condensed sans |
| Colour | Neon red, cyan, magenta on near-black; film teal-green and warm tungsten | Ground #0d0f0e (or teal #12302b); ink #f1e9d8; accents #ff3b4a, #29e3d6, amber #ffb347 |
| Motifs | Neon signs, minibus number boards, tiles, steam, rain, film frames, 囍 | Neon-outline icons, minibus-board tags, tile patterns, letterbox bars |
| Light & depth | Glow, motion blur, colour cast, step-printed smear | Glow on accents only; grain; blur only on small decor |
| Motion | Neon flicker, slow step-printing | One sign flickers now and then; slow fades |
| Breakage | Worn enamel, peeling tiles, a dead neon letter | Local wear; content stays clean |
| Integration | Content lives on signs and menus in the street | Sections are signboards / menu boards; decor is neon and film frames, not stickers |

**Signature** - a vertical neon sign in Traditional Chinese; teal-and-red film grade with grain; cha chaan teng menu boards.
**Never** - flat pastel, clean white minimalism, Simplified-only signage.

## Kinds by function
chat → late-night film subtitles (yellow, letterboxed) · menus / lists → 茶餐厅 menu board · music → jukebox and neon ·
calendar → old wall calendar with lunar dates · journeys → minibus route board.

## Hero and lines
Hero: a night street of stacked neon signs over a tram, or a film still in letterbox. Lines: overhead tram wires, bamboo scaffolding, neon tubes bending round corners, the cha chaan teng menu double rule, rain streaks.
