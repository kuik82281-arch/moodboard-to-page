# Style brief: business (商务风 · 克制专业)

References (typical): annual reports, consulting decks, private-bank brochures, Swiss grids, leather notebooks, a navy suit.

| Dimension | What I see | For the page |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Heavy coated paper, brushed metal, leather, glass desk | Flat surfaces; at most one subtle material (linen card, brushed rule) |
| Edges | Straight, square or tiny radius; hairline rules | Hairline dividers instead of boxes; corners 2-6px |
| Layering | One plane; alignment does the work | Strict grid, no tilt, no overlaps |
| Density | Generous margins, information in tidy blocks | 8pt spacing, big margins, one idea per block |
| Text as texture | Numbers as the ornament: big KPIs, footnotes, section numbers | Oversized figures, "01 / 02" section indices, small-caps labels |
| Typography | Neo-grotesque or a sharp serif display; tabular figures | Display: Didot / Baskerville or Helvetica Neue bold; body: SF / PingFang; tabular-nums |
| Colour | Navy, charcoal, white, one restrained accent (gold, emerald, signal red) | Ground #f7f6f3 or #0f1a2b; ink #1a1a1a; accent only on numbers and one main button |
| Motifs | Charts, rules, monograms, seals, fountain pen | Thin line charts, monogram initials, progress as fine bars |
| Light & depth | Flat, maybe one soft long shadow | Shadows 0 1px 2px at most; no glow |
| Motion | Precise, short ease-out, nothing bouncy | 150-200ms fades and slides |
| Breakage | None | Wholeness everywhere; order is the style |
| Integration | Content set into a grid on the page | Everything printed in the grid; no stickers or pasted objects |

**Signature** - a strict grid with hairlines; numbers as the hero; one accent colour used sparingly.
**Never** - tilts, textures, cute icons, gradients behind text, more than one accent.

## Kinds by function
dashboard → annual report (KPIs, sparklines) · calendar → executive diary (leather band, gold date) · list → memo
(numbered, initialled) · settings → contract (clauses, small-caps headings) · chat → minutes of a meeting.

## Hero and lines
Hero: one large, precise visual - a skyline of bar-chart towers, a fine line chart as horizon, an architectural elevation. Lines stay plain here (hairlines are the style), but give them a job: axis ticks, rulers, ledger rules.
