# Style brief: Western (欧美风 · 杂志 / Americana / Y2K)

欧美 covers several worlds - a fashion magazine, Americana, Scandinavian minimal, Y2K streetwear. Read the references
(or ask) to know which; the table is the editorial default, the variants follow.

| Dimension | What I see | For the page |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Glossy magazine paper, full-bleed photos, white walls | Photo-led; big images bleeding off the edges |
| Edges | Full bleed, hard crops, thin frames | No boxes; crops and white margins define blocks |
| Layering | A headline crossing the photo | One headline over a photo; otherwise clean |
| Density | One huge element, lots of air | One hero per screen; small captions |
| Text as texture | Masthead, cover lines, pull quotes, folios, credits | Masthead title, folios, "photographed by" captions |
| Typography | Didone masthead, serif italics, tracked grotesque labels | Display: Didot / Bodoni 72; italic: Baskerville italic; labels: Helvetica caps, tracked |
| Colour | Black and white with the photo's colour | Ground #fff / #f4f1ec; ink #111; accent taken from the user's photo |
| Motifs | Cover lines, barcode, issue number | Issue numbers, cover-line list, a barcode on the profile |
| Light & depth | Studio light, crisp | No texture; only real photo shadows |
| Motion | Page turns, slow zoom | Slow scale on the hero image |
| Breakage | None | Whole |
| Integration | Text printed on the page and over photos | Content laid out as a spread; no stickers |

**Signature** - a magazine masthead and cover lines; full-bleed photo with a headline over it; tracked-caps captions.
**Never** - cute rounded cards, emoji, clutter.

## Variants
- **Americana** - diner menus, licence plates, motel key tags, red-white-blue, worn enamel; for road trips and journeys.
- **Y2K** - chrome bubbles, baby blue and silver, pixel stars, low-rise lookbook photos; for music and profiles.
- **Scandi minimal** - oatmeal, oak, one black line, lots of air; for reading and settings.

## Kinds by function
profile → magazine cover · diary → feature spread with pull quote · gallery → contact sheet · music → liner-notes spread.

## Hero and lines
Hero: a full-bleed photograph or a huge masthead over it; Americana - a diner sign or a highway at sunset. Lines: film strip perforations, contact-sheet grease-pencil marks, highway lane lines, chrome trims.
