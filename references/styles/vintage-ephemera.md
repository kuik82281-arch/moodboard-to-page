# Style brief: vintage ephemera (复古做旧 · 碎报纸)

References: torn newspaper scraps with a bold serif headline and an engraved flourish; a torn corner of old letter paper
with French copperplate handwriting and an engraved pocket watch; a torn newsprint card of engraved instruments.

| Dimension | What I see | For the page |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Newsprint, kraft, old letter paper, mottled and stained, never clean white | Each block is a different paper: newsprint for lists, letter paper for notes, kraft for labels |
| Edges | Torn on every side, irregular; some corners burnt / scorched darker | Irregular clip-path polygons (several seeds); one or two burnt corners per page, not all |
| Layering | Scraps overlap, slightly tilted, as if pasted in a scrapbook | Rotate scraps -3°..3°, overlap headers onto panels, tape on corners |
| Density | Full: text fills the paper to its edges | Faint text columns behind content; few empty areas |
| Text as texture | Newspaper columns, French handwriting in brown ink | Filler newsprint (7px, alpha .12) and script lines (alpha .15) in the gaps |
| Typography | Heavy newspaper serif caps for titles, copperplate script accents, small typewriter labels | Display: Bodoni 72 / Didot caps; accents: Snell Roundhand / Pinyon Script; labels: American Typewriter |
| Colour | Sepia, cream, tobacco brown, black ink; a little seal red | Ground #e9dcc0, ink #2b211a, sepia #6f4128, accent #8e2b20 |
| Motifs | Engraved clocks, instruments, flourishes, leaves, wax seals | Cut from the references as stickers; one or two per section |
| Light & depth | Printed, flat; shadows only where paper lifts | Soft drop shadow under scraps, no glow |
| Motion | Still; paper might flutter | None, or a slow 1° sway on a corner |
| Breakage | Strong but local: torn edges, burnt corners, missing pieces | Every scrap torn; important numbers on calm paper; never tear through text |
| Integration | Text and pictures printed on the paper; scraps, tickets, flowers pasted on top, loosely | The app is printed on the page (boxed features, ink on the same paper); only scraps and hand marks lie on top |

**Signature** - torn scraps tilted and layered; newspaper serif caps + copperplate script; faint text filling the paper.
**Never** - one uniform crumple over every box; perfect rounded cards; clean white; modern sans headings.

## Hero and lines
Hero: an engraved centrepiece - an old clock face, a botanical plate, a ship in a storm, a map with a compass rose. Lines: telegraph wires, typewriter ribbon, sewing thread with stitches, ruler marks, postal string tied round.
