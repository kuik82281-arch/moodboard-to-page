# Style brief: sweetheart girly (甜心少女 · 糖果与蝴蝶结)

References (typical): Sanrio, Japanese girls' magazines, candy packaging, bows and pearls, lace socks, pink bedrooms, coquette boards.

| Dimension | What I see | For the page |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Glossy candy plastic, satin ribbon, pearls, gingham, glitter, puffy stickers | Glossy puffy buttons, satin bows, gingham fills |
| Edges | Round, scalloped, heart-shaped, lace trims | Big radii, scalloped and lace borders, heart-shaped tags |
| Layering | Stickers stacked, bows on corners, charms dangling | Sticker decor on corners; charms hanging from headers |
| Density | Sweetly busy, but tidy | Decor on the edges, content centred and calm |
| Text as texture | Tiny English labels ("so sweet", "xoxo"), hearts as punctuation | Small script labels, ♡ bullets |
| Typography | Round bubbly sans, cute script, bold rounded display | Display: Arial Rounded / Yuanti SC; script: Snell Roundhand; body: PingFang |
| Colour | Baby pink, strawberry milk, cherry red, cream, a little lilac | Ground #fff4f7; ink #5a3340; accents #ff8fb1, #e8445a, #c9b6ff |
| Motifs | Bows, hearts, cherries, strawberries, pearls, bunnies, stars | Bow on titles, cherry charms, pearl dividers, heart checkboxes |
| Light & depth | Glossy highlights, soft drop shadows, sparkles | Inner highlight on buttons, soft pink shadows, a few sparkles |
| Motion | Bouncy, sparkly | Spring scale on tap, a twinkle |
| Breakage | None | Everything whole and neat |
| Integration | Stickers and charms decorate a tidy page | Content in neat cards; decor pinned to card corners |

**Signature** - bows and pearls; strawberry-milk pink with cherry red; glossy puffy controls.
**Never** - black, grunge, sharp corners, dull colours.

## Kinds by function
diary → a heart diary with a little lock · focus → a candy jar filling with sweets · mood → a mood ring / heart meter ·
music → a pink cassette walkman · calendar → a sticker-sheet month.

## Hero and lines
Hero: one big sweet object - a strawberry cake, a pink vanity table, a candy shop window, worked in glossy detail. Lines: pearl strings, satin ribbon, lace trim, candy-stripe, a chain of tiny hearts, beaded charms.
