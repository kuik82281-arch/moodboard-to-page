# Style brief: punk zine (朋克 · 拼贴小报)

References (typical): photocopied flyers, ransom-note lettering, safety pins, tape, spray paint, grids of torn photos.

| Dimension | What I see | For the page |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Photocopy paper, gaffer tape, cardboard, metal studs, mesh | Blocks as xeroxed sheets; tape strips as separators |
| Edges | Scissor-cut jagged, ripped, staples; hard cuts | Sharp jagged polygons, no soft tears |
| Layering | Chaotic paste-up, overlapping, crooked, layered 3-4 deep | Rotations up to ±6°, deliberate overlaps, elements breaking the grid |
| Density | Crammed, loud | Little empty space; noise and marks in gaps |
| Text as texture | Slogans, scrawls, stencils, cut-out letters | Graffiti scribbles, stamped words behind content |
| Typography | Ransom-note cut letters, condensed grotesque, marker handwriting | Display: mixed cut-out letters (each letter its own box / font); body: condensed sans uppercase |
| Colour | Black, white, one or two loud accents (red, hot pink, acid yellow) | High contrast; accent used in blocks, not gradients |
| Motifs | Safety pins, stars, skulls, X marks, arrows | Stickers + CSS shapes, rotated |
| Light & depth | Flat, xerox grain, misregistration | Halftone, offset duplicate headings, no soft shadows |
| Motion | Jittery, glitchy | Small step animations, flicker on hover |
| Breakage | Everywhere: torn, crossed out, spray-painted over | Break freely, but keep inputs and buttons crisp and readable |
| Integration | Everything is pasted up and then photocopied - pasted *is* the medium | Blocks are pasted-up cut-outs, then flattened by xerox grain; no clean floating cards |

**Signature** - ransom-note headings; xerox black-and-white with one loud accent; crooked paste-up.
**Never** - gradients, soft rounded cards, pastel calm, tidy grids.

## Kinds - pick by what the page is for

One punk kit pasted on every page reads as a production line. Keep the shared bones (photocopy, crooked paste-up,
hard shadows, crisp controls) and change ground, accent, title and wall per page:

| Kind | Fits | Ground / accent | Title | Wall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stencil · DIY gym / hardcore | timers, goals, workouts, discipline | concrete grey; safety orange + hazard yellow | sprayed through a stencil (bridges, overspray) | hazard tape, sprayed arrow, drips, motto sprayed big and faint |
| Riot grrrl diary zine | feelings, diary, letters | lined notebook with red margin; hot pink + red marker | ransom-note cut-outs | marker hearts crossed out, safety pin, star, motto on a torn notebook strip |
| Gig flyer · wheatpaste | music, events, lists of things | newsprint / kraft; two-colour riso red + blue | wood type printed out of register | torn bills with huge words, halftone record, staples, round stamp |
| Xerox newsletter | reading, news, long text | white copy paper; black + one highlighter | typewriter caps, underlined by hand | columns, redaction bars, arrows, page numbers |
| Skate / sticker bomb | collections, galleries, home grids | grip-tape black; neon green + pink | bubble graffiti outline | overlapping die-cut stickers, checkerboard |

## Hero and lines
Hero: one huge paste-up per page - a gig poster collage, a stencilled giant number, a spray-painted portrait in halftone. Lines: barbed wire, safety-pin chains, tape strips, staples in a row, marker underlines, zip teeth.
