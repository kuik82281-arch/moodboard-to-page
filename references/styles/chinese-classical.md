# Style brief: Chinese classical (中华风 · 古典)

References (typical): rice paper and silk, ink-wash landscapes, lattice windows, seal stamps, vertical calligraphy, celadon.

| Dimension | What I see | For the page |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Rice paper, silk, lacquered wood, porcelain, jade | Panels as rice paper or silk scrolls; frames as lacquer or lattice |
| Edges | Scroll ends, mounted borders, lattice frames, round moon gates, fret (回纹) borders | Double-rule borders, scroll caps on long panels, circular / begonia windows |
| Layering | Ordered, symmetric, framed views (借景) | Centred compositions; content seen through frames |
| Density | Generous blank space (留白) balanced by dense details | Wide empty ground; ornament concentrated at borders |
| Text as texture | Calligraphy, vertical poems, seals | Faint vertical calligraphy lines; red seals as accents |
| Typography | Song / Kai, brush calligraphy, vertical setting | Display: Kaiti / brush; body: Songti; vertical headings where it fits |
| Colour | Ink black and greys, vermilion, indigo, celadon, gold | Ground #f3eee2, ink #2a2622, accent #b0342b, celadon #8fb3a3 |
| Motifs | Clouds, plum / bamboo / orchid, lanterns, cranes, waves, fret patterns | Ink-style stickers, fret borders, seal stamps |
| Light & depth | Flat ink, subtle silk sheen | No drop shadows; mounting borders give depth |
| Motion | Slow ink-wash spreading, unrolling scrolls | Fade / unroll reveals |
| Breakage | None: wholeness, balance and symmetry | Never tear; asymmetry only in ink-wash placement |
| Integration | Written or painted into the paper and mounted in frames; seals stamped into the ink | Content is written into scrolls and framed views; seals stamped on, never stickers |

**Signature** - 留白 with framed views; Song / Kai and vertical text; vermilion seals on ink and rice paper.
**Never** - torn grunge, neon, round cute stickers, crowded layouts.

## Hero and lines
Hero: a mountain-and-water landscape scroll, a moon gate onto a garden, a pavilion in the rain. Lines: brush strokes with dry ends, plum branches, bamboo stalks with nodes, lattice (冰裂纹), the 回纹 fret, threads of a guqin.
