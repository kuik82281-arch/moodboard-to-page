# Techniques

Building blocks for turning a brief into CSS. Pick by the brief; none of them is the style by itself.

## Edges

- **Irregular silhouettes** (torn, burnt, scissor, deckle): a `clip-path: polygon(...)` generated by
  `scripts/edge_shapes.py clip --kind torn --seed 3` - several seeds, used in turn so no two boxes share an edge.
  `clip-path` cuts shadows too: put the shadow on a wrapper with `filter: drop-shadow()` only on small elements, or fake
  it with a darker copy behind.
- **Burnt rim**: the same polygon as a PNG mask (`edge_shapes.py mask --kind burnt`) plus an inner gradient from the
  ground colour to dark brown / black along the rim (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 18px #3a2414` approximates it on rectangles).
- **Scallops / stamps**: `mask: radial-gradient(circle at ... , transparent r, #000 r+0.5px)` repeated along the edge.
- **Round soft** (Korean): large radii (20-28px), 1px light inner border, soft long shadow; never jagged.
- **Frames** (Chinese): double rule borders, fret (回纹) border images, seal-red square corners.

## Texture without heavy images

- **Newsprint / text-as-texture**: a pseudo-element with long filler text in the style's language and font, small
  (6-9px), low alpha (0.08-0.2), `overflow: hidden`, sometimes columns (`column-count`) or rotated; or an SVG data URI
  pattern of short grey bars for "unreadable" columns.
- **Paper grain / mottling**: one tileable 512 px WebP made once (noise + soft blotches); never a full-screen PNG.
- **Halftone**: `radial-gradient(circle, ink 30%, transparent 31%) 0 0 / 6px 6px` with a mask fading it.
- **Xerox / misregistration** (punk): duplicate a heading with `::before` in another colour offset 2-3 px; high
  contrast, `filter: contrast()` only on small images.
- **Rice paper / silk** (Chinese): very light fibre texture, ink-wash gradients in greys, lots of empty ground.

## Layering

- Give scraps different small rotations (-3° ... 3°) with `rotate`, not `transform`, so they compose with others.
- Tape / pins / clips as small stickers on the corners, also rotated.
- Overlap neighbours slightly (negative margins or grid overlap) where the style layers; keep the readable core flat.
- A decoration that must sit above content needs its own element outside any `z-index: -1` / `position: fixed`
  background layer; pseudo-elements cannot leave their stacking context.

## Typography

Pick by personality; always give a fallback stack that keeps the personality.

| Personality | Stack (device / bundled first) |
|---|---|
| Newspaper headline | "Bodoni 72", Didot, "Playfair Display", "Times New Roman", serif |
| Old book serif | Baskerville, "Hoefler Text", Georgia, serif |
| Copperplate script | "Snell Roundhand", "Savoye LET", "Pinyon Script", cursive |
| Typewriter | "American Typewriter", "Courier New", monospace |
| Punk display | Impact, "Arial Black", "Helvetica Neue" condensed, sans-serif (+ uppercase, tight tracking) |
| Round soft | "SF Pro Rounded", "Nunito", "PingFang SC", sans-serif |
| Chinese classical | "Songti SC", "STSong", "Kaiti SC", "STKaiti", serif (vertical: `writing-mode: vertical-rl`) |

Display type carries the style; body type stays readable. Ask before downloading web fonts.

## Performance (phones)

Large `mix-blend-mode`, `filter`, `backdrop-filter`, SVG turbulence and many big images crash or stall mobile Safari.
Bake effects into small images, reuse them, keep animations on `transform` / `opacity`.

## Illustration (the hero)

When the hero cannot be cut from the references, generate it as an SVG with a small Python script - see
`scripts/examples/paris_etching.py` (Eiffel Tower, quai, bridge, Seine, plus string lights, a balcony tile and a lamp).
What makes it read as art and not clip-art:

- **Structure by procedure**: lattice by X-bracing between two edge curves, rows of windows, balusters, ripples -
  loops, not hand-placed shapes. A profile function (e.g. `half_width(t) = a + b*(1-t)^3`) gives organic curves.
- **Tone by hatching**: fine parallel strokes (0.3-0.6px, low alpha), denser for shadow, broken at random; cross-hatch
  only in the darkest places.
- **A watercolour wash under the lines**: two or three gradients (sky, sun, water) at low alpha; the etching on top.
- **Depth**: far (buildings, trees), middle (the subject), near (a bridge, a railing) crossing in front.
- **Fade into the page**: an SVG mask at the bottom and a CSS mask at the open side, so it belongs to the ground.
- **Scale it up without thick lines**: `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` on every stroke keeps etched lines
  hair-thin when the picture is enlarged to fill a whole page.
- Size: aim for under ~300 KB of SVG; it compresses well.
- **As the page itself**: `background: url(hero.svg) 72% 0 / auto max(1250px, 140vw) no-repeat` on the page root - the
  picture fills the page and scrolls with it; content is set into it.

## Containers as objects

- **Folded letter**: two crease gradients (dark hairline + white highlight) at 33% / 66%, deckled `clip-path`, a wax seal.
- **Sticky note**: pastel gradient, a darker 14px glue strip at the top, a lifted corner (`border-bottom-right-radius:
  26px 6px`), a soft shadow under the curl, turned 2-3°.
- **Bill / receipt**: typewriter font, a dashed header with the shop's name, ruled lines, a coffee-cup ring
  (`radial-gradient` annulus), a zigzag tear (`mask` with `conic-gradient` teeth), a handwritten "merci".
- **Atmosphere behind text**: a `radial-gradient(closest-side, scene-colour 74%, transparent)` mist sized ~125% of the
  block - soft, never a rectangle.

## Lines as objects

- **Musical staff progress**: track = five lines from `repeating-linear-gradient` (0.7px every 4px, 17px tall), a clef
  as a small inline SVG at the start, the fill a varied melody tile (`repeat-x`, ~72px wide, mixed notes) whose width is
  the value, closed by a double bar `::after`.
- **Overhead / string lights**: a catenary (`Q` curve) with hanging bulbs and a radial glow; repeat with
  `background-repeat: round` so whole swags fit any width.
- **Railings**: a wrought-iron or balustrade tile repeated along the bottom of a section.
- **Postal**: perforated stamp (`mask: radial-gradient` holes), round postmark plus wavy cancellation lines.
